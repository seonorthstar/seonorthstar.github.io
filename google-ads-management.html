SEO or Google Ads?

The honest difference

They work very differently. Here’s what each one is good for.

Fast

Google Ads

  • You can show at the top of Google within days
  • You pay for every click, so it stops when you stop paying
  • Great for urgent jobs and quiet weeks

Slow, then steady

SEO

  • Takes months to build up
  • Keeps bringing in work without paying per click
  • Lowers what you need to spend on ads over time

My honest take: Ads bring calls now. SEO brings cheaper calls later. If Ads aren’t worth it for your business, I’ll tell you on our first call.

How much should I spend on ads?

I recommend at least £500–£750 a month. Below that, there usually aren’t enough clicks to judge what’s working for two to three months.

Who owns the Google Ads account?

You do. You pay Google directly, and I get manager access. If we stop working together, everything stays with you.

Do you take a cut of my ad spend?

No. My fee is £199 a month, whatever you spend. I never mark up your ad budget.

How fast will I get calls?

Ads can show from the day they go live. The first few weeks are about learning which searches bring paying customers, so expect results to improve over the first two to three months.

What are Local Services Ads?

They’re ads at the very top of Google for trades like plumbers and electricians. You only pay when a customer gets in touch. You’ll need the right trade checks and insurance to qualify.

Am I tied in?

No. It’s a 30-day rolling agreement. Cancel any time.

Do I need SEO as well?

Not to start. But Ads stop when you stop paying, and SEO keeps working. Most businesses do best with both. I’ll be honest about what’s right for you.
