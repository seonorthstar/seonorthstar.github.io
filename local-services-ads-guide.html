What are Local Services Ads?

Local Services Ads are Google’s pay-per-lead ads for local businesses. They sit at the very top of the results, above the normal ads and the map pack, and show your business name, star rating, a Google badge and a call button.

The big difference from normal Google Ads: you don’t pay when someone clicks. You pay when a customer calls, messages or books with you through the ad.

The badges: Google Guaranteed and Google Screened

  • Google Guaranteed is for home service trades, such as plumbers, electricians, roofers and locksmiths.
  • Google Screened is for professional services, such as solicitors, accountants and financial planners.
  • Licence checks apply in some trades, so Google can show that the business holds the right licence.

To get a badge, Google runs checks on your business. These can include background checks, licence checks and proof of insurance, depending on your trade.

How much do Local Services Ads cost in the UK?

You set a weekly budget and pay per lead. What a lead costs depends on your trade and area:

If a lead is clearly not valid, such as spam, a wrong number or a job you don’t offer, you can dispute it and ask Google for a credit.

Are they worth it for a small business?

  • They work best when you are a trade that wins work by phone, you answer calls quickly, and you already have good Google reviews.
  • They work less well when you have few reviews, you can’t pick up the phone during the day, or your jobs are small compared to the lead price.

Your reviews and your Google Business Profile matter a lot here. Ads with more good reviews tend to win more leads, so local SEO and Local Services Ads help each other.

How to get started

  • Check your trade and area are covered on Google’s Local Services Ads sign-up page.
  • Have your insurance and any licences ready for the checks.
  • Get your Google Business Profile complete and ask happy customers for reviews.
  • Set a small weekly budget, then track which leads turn into paid jobs.

Local Services Ads or Google Ads?

Local Services Ads are simple and pay-per-lead. Google Ads give you more control over keywords, wording and the page people land on. If you want help with Google Ads, see my Google Ads management page. If you want to show up in the free map results too, see local SEO services.

Google changes how these ads work from time to time, and some changes start in the US first. Last checked: November 2026.

What are Google Local Services Ads?

They are ads that show at the very top of Google for local trades and services, above the normal ads and the map. They carry a Google badge, show your reviews, and you pay per lead rather than per click.

How much do Local Services Ads cost in the UK?

You pay for each lead, not each click. UK estimates for 2026 put plumbers and electricians at around £25–£45 a lead, locksmiths at £40–£60 and painters at £15–£30. Prices are higher in London and big cities, and you set a weekly budget.

Do I pay for every call?

You are charged when a customer calls, messages or books through the ad. If a lead is clearly wrong, such as spam or a job you don’t do, you can dispute it with Google.

What is the difference between Google Guaranteed and Google Screened?

Google Guaranteed is for home service trades like plumbers and roofers. Google Screened is for professional services like solicitors and accountants. Both mean Google has run checks on the business, such as background and licence checks.

Are Local Services Ads better than Google Ads?

Not always. Local Services Ads suit trades that want calls and have good reviews. Google Ads gives more control over keywords and landing pages. Many businesses do best with a strong Google Business Profile first, then one or both ads.

Can I get Local Services Ads in my area?

It depends on your trade and location. Google only offers them for certain categories in certain areas, and the list changes. The quickest way to check is Google’s own sign-up page.
